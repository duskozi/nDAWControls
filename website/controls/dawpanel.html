Controls / Mixer and layout

DawPanel

A titled panel in the skin colours, with header tools and a hide button.

<daw:DawPanel>
SkinGraphite

#Overview

DawPanel is a HeaderedContentControl that groups controls under a title. Tools adds content to the right side of the header, such as a bypass button or a preset picker. The × button (or IsShown="False") collapses the panel.

Use it for

  • sections of a synth or effect editor, docked tool panels, sidebars

#Quick start

XAML
<daw:DawPanel Header="INSTRUMENT" CanHide="True" IsShown="{Binding ShowInstrument, Mode=TwoWay}">
    <daw:DawPanel.Tools>
        <daw:LedToggleButton Content="BYPASS" Height="18"/>
    </daw:DawPanel.Tools>
    <WrapPanel Margin="8">
        <daw:Knob Label="Attack"/>
        <daw:Knob Label="Release"/>
    </WrapPanel>
</daw:DawPanel>

#Examples

#A plain panel

XAML
<daw:DawPanel ShowHeader="False" Padding="12">
    <TextBlock Text="No title bar"/>
</daw:DawPanel>

#XAML of the screenshot

This is the exact XAML of the screenshot at the top. The gallery app shows it live, so you can edit it and press Ctrl+Enter to see your change.

<daw:DawPanel Header="INSTRUMENT" Width="300" Height="160" CanHide="True">

    </WrapPanel>
</daw:DawPanel>

#Tips

A hidden panel is collapsed: its controls do no layout and no drawing, and meters inside stop costing CPU. Use this for parts of your UI the user can turn off.

#API reference

#Properties

PropertyTypeDefaultDescription
IsShownbooltrueFalse = collapsed (no layout, no rendering, no data).
CanHidebooltrueShow the hide (×) button in the header.
ShowHeaderbooltrueWhether the title bar with the hide button is shown.
ToolsobjectnullExtra header content, right-aligned before the hide button (pickers, toggles).

#Methods

MethodDescription
Hide()Hides the panel (used by the header button; also handy from code).
