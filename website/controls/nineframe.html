Controls / Mixer and layout

NineFrame

A drop-in replacement for Border with bevels, drawn by the same renderer as the controls.

<daw:NineFrame>
SkinGraphite

#Overview

NineFrame has the properties of Border (Background, BorderBrush, BorderThickness, CornerRadius, Padding) plus Bevel: Flat, Raised, Sunken or Glow. It draws with the native nine-patch renderer, so your own panels, displays and buttons match the controls exactly, and equal frames share one cached bitmap.

Use it for

  • your own LCD displays, panels, cards and button templates that should look like the built-in controls

#Quick start

XAML
<daw:NineFrame Bevel="Sunken" CornerRadius="3" BorderThickness="1" Padding="6,2"
               Background="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.LcdBackground}}"
               BorderBrush="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.LcdBorder}}">
    <TextBlock Text="120.0 BPM" Foreground="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.LcdText}}"
               FontFamily="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.LcdFontFamily}}"/>
</daw:NineFrame>

#Examples

#In your own button template

XAML
<ControlTemplate TargetType="Button">
    <daw:NineFrame x:Name="frame" Bevel="Raised" CornerRadius="3" BorderThickness="1" Padding="10,3"
                   Background="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.ButtonBackground}}"
                   BorderBrush="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.ButtonBorder}}">
        <ContentPresenter HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center"/>
    </daw:NineFrame>
    <ControlTemplate.Triggers>
        <Trigger Property="IsPressed" Value="True">
            <Setter TargetName="frame" Property="Bevel" Value="Sunken"/>
            <Setter TargetName="frame" Property="Background"
                    Value="{DynamicResource {x:Static daw:DawSkinKeys.ButtonPressed}}"/>
        </Trigger>
    </ControlTemplate.Triggers>
</ControlTemplate>

#Tips

The renderer is used for a uniform border with one corner radius, and solid or two-stop vertical gradient brushes. Anything else (different widths per side, other brushes) is drawn like a normal Border, so it always works.

#API reference

#Properties

PropertyTypeDefaultDescription
BackgroundBrushfrom skinFill: a solid colour or a two-stop vertical gradient becomes the nine-patch.
BorderThicknessThickness—Outline width in pixels; a different width per side is drawn like Border.
CornerRadiusCornerRadius—Corner radius in pixels.
PaddingThickness—Space between the frame and its child.
BevelFrameStyleFlatShading of the generated frame.
