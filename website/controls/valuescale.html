Controls / Metering

ValueScale

Tick marks and labels for a fader or meter, using the same taper so the ticks line up exactly.

<daw:ValueScale>
SkinGraphite

#Overview

ValueScale draws ticks and labels for the values you list in Ticks. Give it the same Minimum, Maximum and Taper as the control next to it, and use Padding to match where that control's travel starts and ends.

Use it for

  • dB scales next to faders and meters, frequency or percent scales next to horizontal controls

#Quick start

XAML
<daw:ValueScale Placement="End" Minimum="-60" Maximum="0" Taper="Decibel" MinimumText="-∞"
                Ticks="0 -3 -6 -12 -20 -30 -60" LabelFormat="{}{0:0}"/>

#Examples

#Horizontal scale

XAML
<daw:ValueScale Orientation="Horizontal" Placement="Start" Minimum="0" Maximum="100" Height="22"
                Ticks="0 25 50 75 100" LabelFormat="{}{0}%"/>

#XAML of the screenshot

This is the exact XAML of the screenshot at the top. The gallery app shows it live, so you can edit it and press Ctrl+Enter to see your change.

XAMLGallery: Fader with scale and meter
<StackPanel Orientation="Horizontal">
    <daw:ValueScale Placement="End" Padding="0,20,0,20" Minimum="-60" Maximum="0" Taper="Decibel"
                    MinimumText="-∞" Ticks="0 -3 -6 -12 -20 -30 -60"/>
    <daw:Fader x:Name="fader" Width="30" Height="240" Taper="Decibel" Minimum="-60" Maximum="0" Value="-4"
               ShowValueText="False" MinimumText="-∞"/>
    <daw:LevelMeter x:Name="meter" Width="8" Margin="4,20,0,20"/>
</StackPanel>

#Tips

  • Placement is the side the ticks are on: Start, End or Both.
  • Fader caps are 40 pixels high, so Padding="0,20,0,20" lines a vertical scale up with a fader of the same height.

#Properties

PropertyTypeDefaultDescription
Minimumdouble-70Value at the bottom (or left) end.
Maximumdouble6Value at the top (or right) end.
TaperValueTaperLinearMapping from value to position; use the same taper as the control the scale belongs to.
TicksDoubleCollectionnullValues to label, e.g. Ticks="6 0 -6 -12 -24 -48 -70".
OrientationOrientationVerticalVertical (default) or horizontal.
PlacementScaleTickPlacementStartSide of the scale the ticks are on.
TickLengthdouble4Length of the tick marks in pixels.
LabelFormatstring"{0:0}"Format of the labels, for example "{0:0}" or "{0:0.0} dB".
MinimumTextstringnullLabel used for a tick at Minimum (e.g. "-∞").
