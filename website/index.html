WPF controls for DAW and MIDI apps

Knobs, faders, meters, a mixer channel strip, a transport bar, a piano keyboard, a piano roll and a step sequencer. Real-time ready, five live-switchable skins, and plain WPF you already know.

Get started Browse the controls
>dotnet add package nDAWControls
SkinGraphite

Everything a music app needs

The controls come from nMIDI Editor, where they draw the mixer, the piano roll and the keyboard. They are tested in a real app.

21 controls

Knobs, faders, meters, channel strips, transport, piano keyboard, piano roll, controller lane, step sequencer, XY pad, envelope editor, wheels and pedals.

Five skins, switched live

Graphite, Studio, Light classic, Retro hardware and High contrast. One line of code changes the whole app, or just one panel.

Built for real-time audio

Meters and MIDI lights take values from any thread without locks, and draw once per frame with the newest data.

Mouse, wheel and keyboard

Everything takes focus. Arrow keys, double-click to default, Shift for fine steps, computer-keyboard piano.

Fast drawing

A native nine-patch renderer draws the frames, and equal frames share one cached bitmap. Long songs stay smooth.

Plain WPF

Lookless controls with dependency properties, routed events and commands. Bind them in MVVM like any WPF control.

Four lines to a working panel

Add the package, add the daw namespace, and use the controls in XAML. Value controls are WPF range controls, so Value, Minimum, Maximum and binding work as you expect. Meters take levels from any thread.

Read the getting-started guide →

XAMLMainWindow.xaml
<Window xmlns:daw="urn:ndaw-controls" ...>
    <StackPanel Orientation="Horizontal">
        <daw:Knob Label="Cutoff" Taper="Log" Minimum="20" Maximum="20000" Value="{Binding Cutoff}"
                  ValueFormatter="{x:Static daw:ValueFormatters.Frequency}"/>
        <daw:Fader Height="200" Taper="Decibel" Minimum="-60" Maximum="6" Value="{Binding VolumeDb}"/>
        <daw:LevelMeter x:Name="meter" Width="8"/>
        <daw:LedToggleButton Role="Mute" Content="M" IsChecked="{Binding IsMuted}"/>
    </StackPanel>
</Window>
C#MainWindow.xaml.cs
meter.PushLevels(peak, rms);           // from your audio thread, no lock needed
SkinManager.Apply(DawSkin.Studio);     // switch the skin of the whole app

The controls

Each control page has a screenshot in every skin, XAML and C# examples, the mouse and keyboard handling, and the full API.

KnobA rotary control with a value ring, a label and a value readout. The workhorse of every synth and effect panel.FaderA slider with a moving cap, vertical or horizontal, with a decibel curve for mixer channels.ValueBoxA compact numeric field: drag or scroll it like a knob, or double-click and type a value.XYPadTwo parameters at once on a square pad, such as filter cutoff and resonance.EnvelopeEditorA graphical ADSR envelope: drag the handles to set attack, decay, sustain and release.LevelMeterA peak and RMS meter with peak hold and a clip light, fed lock-free from your audio thread.ValueScaleTick marks and labels for a fader or meter, using the same taper so the ticks line up exactly.MidiActivityMonitorMIDI activity lights per channel or per track: notes, controllers, held notes and the last message.LedToggleButtonA latching button that lights up when on: mute, solo, record arm, monitor and your own toggles.TransportButtonA single transport button with a built-in icon: play, stop, record, loop, metronome and more.TransportBarA complete transport in one control: buttons, position display, tempo and time signature.TimeDisplayAn LCD readout of a song position, as bars, beats and ticks or as minutes and seconds.ChannelStripA complete mixer channel: name, pan, fader with a dB scale, stereo meter, mute, solo and arm.TrackHeaderThe header of a track in an arranger or track list: colour, number, name, mute, solo, output and activity.DawPanelA titled panel in the skin colours, with header tools and a hide button.NineFrameA drop-in replacement for Border with bevels, drawn by the same renderer as the controls.PianoKeyboardA playable piano keyboard in three styles, with velocity, highlights, finger numbers and computer-keyboard play.PerformanceWheelHardware-style pitch-bend and modulation wheels.PianoPedalsSoft, sostenuto and sustain pedals that show half-pedalling and can be pressed with the mouse.PianoRollA fast note editor with a keyboard gutter, bar ruler, snapping, selection, zoom and a playhead.ControllerLaneVelocity, controller and pitch-bend editing under a piano roll, following its scroll, zoom and playhead.StepSequencerA drum-machine grid with velocity per step, row labels, groups and a playhead.
